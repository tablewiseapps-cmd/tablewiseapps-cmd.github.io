**Last updated:** 21 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data tablewise handles and why. It covers
three things:

1. this website, `tablewiseapps-cmd.github.io`;
2. the tablewise LinkedIn app, which we use only to publish our own posts;
3. the tablewise tools (Actors) on the Apify platform.

tablewise is an independent software developer. To contact us about privacy,
open an issue in our
[examples repository](https://github.com/tablewiseapps-cmd/apify-scrapers-examples/issues).
Please don't post personal data in a public issue: say that it's a privacy
request and we'll agree on a private channel there.

## 1. This website

- The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. We don't use cookies, analytics or
  tracking scripts.
- GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address to run and secure
  the service. See the
  [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
- Some links to Apify may carry our affiliate code. If you follow one, Apify
  may set a cookie on apify.com to credit a sign-up to us. That cookie
  belongs to Apify and is covered by the
  [Apify Privacy Policy](https://docs.apify.com/legal/privacy-policy).

## 2. The tablewise LinkedIn app

**What it does.** It lets us publish posts that we write and approve one by
one to our own LinkedIn presence (the tablewise page and its administrator's
profile). It does nothing else.

**What it accesses.** Only what LinkedIn requires to publish a post on
behalf of the person who authorizes the app: an access token and that
person's basic profile identifier.

**What it does not do.** It doesn't read, collect or store data about any
other LinkedIn member: no feeds, connections, messages, comments or profile
data. It doesn't comment, react, connect or send messages. The data is never
sold, shared or used for advertising.

**Storage.** The access token is kept only on the computer of the person who
authorized the app, and expires as LinkedIn sets. It can be revoked at any
time in LinkedIn: *Settings > Data privacy > Permitted services*.

## 3. The tablewise tools on Apify

- Our tools run on Apify's infrastructure, inside the account of the person
  who runs them. The input and the results are stored in that person's Apify
  account, under the [Apify Terms](https://docs.apify.com/legal) and the
  [Apify Privacy Policy](https://docs.apify.com/legal/privacy-policy).
- We don't keep copies of your inputs or results, and our tools don't share
  data between the runs of different users.
- Apify may show us technical information about runs of our tools (for
  example status, duration and error logs). We use it only to keep the tools
  working.
- The tools collect only information that is publicly available without
  logging in. Whoever runs a tool is responsible for having a lawful basis
  to use the results.
- If your public data appears in results and you want it removed, the
  results belong to the person who ran the tool, not to us. We'll still help:
  contact us as described above.

## Your rights

You can ask us what personal data we hold about you, and ask us to correct
or delete it. Since we keep almost none, most requests are answered by
Apify, GitHub or LinkedIn under their own policies, and we'll point you to
the right place.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
